# USB-C cable plug

**Status:** printed (cutout and recess worked with the cables tried)

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Plug shell | 8.25 x 2.4, inserts 6.6 mm |
| Overmold | typically 12 x 6.5 - some cables are bigger |

Wall cutout 10 x 4.5 plus an outside recess 13 x 7.5 that leaves a 0.8 mm wall: the plug then
engages ~5.8 of 6.6 mm. With a plain 2 mm wall only ~4.6 mm, and some cables won't latch.
